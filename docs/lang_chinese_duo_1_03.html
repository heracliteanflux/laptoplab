1.3 - Discuss professions

1.3 - Discuss professions#

Britain/England               yīng  guó           英国🇬🇧
British/English person        yīng  guó   rén     英国人🇬🇧

Chinese language (written)    zhōng wén           中文🇨🇳
doctor                        yī    shēng         医生🧑‍⚕️
English language (written)    yīng  wén           英文🇺🇸🇬🇧
lawyer                        lùü   shī           律师
student                       xué   shēng         学生🧑‍🎓
teacher                       lǎo   shī           老师🧑‍🏫
you                           nǐ                  你

yes; right; correct           duì                 对✅
yes?; right?; correct?        duì   bú    duì?    对不对？

to speak                      shuō                说

对，我是学生。duì, wǒ shì xuéshēng Yes, I'm a student.
你是中国老师，对不对？nǐ shì zhōngguó lǎoshī, duìbúduì? You're a Chinese teacher, right?
你说中文和英文，对不对？nǐ shuō zhōngwén hé yīngwén, duìbúduì? You speak Chinese and English, right?
我是英国老师。wǒ shì yīngguó lǎoshī I'm an English teacher.
我是医生，你呢？wǒ shì yīshēng, nǐ ne? I'm a doctor, how about you?
