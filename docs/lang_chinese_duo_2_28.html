2.28 - Ask for directions#

close/near (to)                                  jìn                  近
downtown; city center                            shì  zhōng xīn       市中心
forward; (straight) ahead; "toward the front"    wǎng qián            往前
from; to; away???                                lí                   离
inward; further in; "toward the inside"          wǎng lǐ              往里
kilometer                                        yì   qiān mǐ         一千米
left                                             zuǒ                  左⬅️
leftward; "to the left"                          wǎng zuǒ             往左🫲
main street                                      dà   jiē             大街
map                                              dì   tú              地图🗺️
meter                                            mǐ                   米 (qī shí mǐ 七十米 seventy meters)
near(by); in the vicinity                        fù   jìn             附近
Peking University                                běi  jīng dà  xué    北京大学
right                                            yòu                  右➡️
rightward; "toward the right"                    wǎng yòu             往右🫱
street; avenue                                   jiē                  街 (yì tiáo jiē 一条街 a street)
thousand                                         qiān                 千 (yì qiān 一千 one thousand)
Tiananmen Square                                 tiān ān   mén        天安门
university                                       dà   xué             大学
you're welcome; "don't / no need to thank me"    bú   yòng xiè        不用谢

to get/take a cab/taxi                           dǎ   chē             打车🚖
to recommend                                     tuī  jiàn            推荐
to turn/take                                     zhuǎn                转
to walk; to move; to go; to leave                zǒu                  走

had better zuì hǎo 最好
how does one go/get (to)? zěn me zǒu? 怎么走？

bú kè qì 不客气 "don't / no need to be so polite"

bú yòng xiè 不用谢 "don't / no need to thank me"

lǐ        里   in(side); within

lǐ   miàn 里面 the inside area; interior (a location)

wǎng lǐ   往里 toward the inside (a directional motion)

zǒu    走   to walk; to move; to go; to leave

zǒu lù 走路 to walk (as a form of movement or transportation)

sàn bù 散步 to take a walk; to go for a stroll
