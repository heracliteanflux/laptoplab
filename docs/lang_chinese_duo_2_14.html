out of town                                    zài   wài  dì           在外地
ping pong / table tennis                       pīng  pāng qiú          乒乓球🏓

the day after tomorrow in the afternoon        hòu   tiān xià wǔ       后天下午

the day after tomorrow in the evening/night    hòu   tiān wǎn shàng    后天晚上

bié chūmén 别出门。Don't go out.
bié jiārù tāmen 别加入他们。Don't join them.
bié páshān 别爬山。Don't go hiking.
bié qù jiàn tā 别去见他。Don't go see/meet him.

hǎoā wǒ xǐhuan dǎ bàngqiú 好啊我喜欢打棒球。Sure I like playing baseball.
hǎoā wǒ yǒukòngr 好啊我有空儿。Sure I'm available.
hǎoā wǒmen jiārù nǐmen 好啊我们加入你们。Sure we'll join y'all.
hǎoā wǒmen xīngqītiān qù pǎobù bā 好啊我们星期天去跑步吧。Sure, let's go for a run on Sunday.

nǐ jǐnzhāng ma? 你紧张吗？Are you nervous?
nǐ míngtiān wǎnshàng yào jiàn tā ma? 你明天晚上要见他吗？Are you going to see/meet her tomorrow night?
nǐ míngtiān zài jiā zuòfàn ma? 你明天在家做饭吗？Are you cooking at home tomorrow?
nǐ xiǎng wǒ ma? 你想我吗？Do you miss me?
nǐ xiǎng xué fǎyǔ ma? 你想学法语吗？Do you want to study French?
nǐ xiǎng zài jiā liànxí jítā ma? 你想在家练习吉他吗？Do you want to practice guitar at home?
nǐ yào qù páshān háishì pǎobù? 你要去爬山还是跑步？Are you going hiking or running?
nǐ yīnggāi duō fùxí 你应该多复习。You should review more.

nǐmen hòutiān zuò shénme? 你们后天做什么？What are y'all doing the day after tomorrow?
nǐmen tèbié xǐhuan diàoyú, duìbúduì? 你们特别喜欢钓鱼，对不对？Y'all especially like fishing, don't you?
nǐmen zhège zhōumò yào qù wàidì ma? 你们这个周末要去外地吗？Are y'all going out of town this weekend?

tā bù xiǎng chūmén 她不想出门。She doesn't want to go out.
tā jīntiān xiàwǔ yào zuò gōngjiāochē 他今天下午要坐公交车。He will take the bus this afternoon.

tāmen dōu shì wǒ de lǎopéngyǒu 他们都是我的老朋友。They are all my old friends.
tāmen jīntiān yào qù páshān 他们今天要去爬山。They are going hiking today.

wǒ bù xiǎng xué gāngqín 我不想学钢琴。I don't want to learn piano.
wǒ de lǎopéngyǒu zài wàidì shēnghuó 我的老朋友在外地生活。My old friend lives out of town.
wǒ hěn jǐnzhāng 我很紧张。I'm really stressed.
wǒ hěn máng, wǒ jīntiān wǎnshàng yào fùxí 我很忙，我今天晚上要复习。I'm very busy, I will review tonight.
wǒ hòutiān qù dǎ lánqiú 我后天去打篮球。I'm going to play basketball the day after tomorrow.
wǒ hòutiān yào fùxí 我后天要复习。I will review the day after tomorrow.
wǒ huì diàoyú 我会钓鱼。I can fish.
wǒ kěyǐ gěi nǐ dǎ diànhuà ma? 我可以给你打电话吗？Can I call you?
wǒ míngtiān qù xué yóuyǒng 我明天去学游泳。I'm going to learn to swim tomorrow.
wǒ míngtiān xiàwǔ qù nǎinai jiā 我明天下午去奶奶家。I'll go to grandma's house tomorrow afternoon. (I'm going to grandma's house tomorrow afternoon.)
wǒ míngtiān yào qù wàidì 我明天要去外地。I will (/ intend to) be out of town tomorrow.
wǒ míngtiān zài jiā xiūxi 我明天在家休息。I'll rest at home tomorrow. (I'm resting at home tomorrow.)
wǒ xiàge xīngqī yào kǎoshì 我下个星期要考试。I will have a test next week.
wǒ xiàge xīngqīliù zài jiā tīng yīnyuè 我下个星期六在家听音乐。I'll be at home listening to music next Saturday. (I'm listening to music at home next Saturday.)
wǒ xiàge xīngqītiān chūmén 我下个星期天出门。I'll go out next Sunday. (I'm going out next Sunday.)
wǒ xiàge xīngqītiān xué jítā 我下个星期天学吉他。I'm learning guitar next Sunday.
wǒ yě xiǎng tā 我也想她。I also miss her.
wǒ zhège xīngqīliù bù chūmén 我这个星期六不出门。I'm not going out this Saturday.
wǒ zhège xīngqītiān yào qù běijīng 我这个星期天要去北京。I will go to Beijing this Sunday.

wǒmen bù xiǎng kǎoshì 我们不想考试。We don't want to take the test.
wǒmen dǎ diànhuà bā 我们打电话吧。Let's make a call.
wǒmen hòutiān xiàwǔ qù tī zúqiú bā 我们后天下午去踢足球吧 Let's go play soccer the day after tomorrow in the afternoon.
wǒmen míngtiān xiàwǔ yào cānguān xiàoyuán 我们明天下午要参观校园。We're visiting campus tomorrow afternoon.
wǒmen míngtiān yǒushìr 我们明天有事儿。We're busy tomorrow.

xiàge xīngqī wǒ hěn máng 下个星期我很忙。I'm very busy next week.
xiàge xīngqītiān de tiānqì bù hǎo 下个星期天的天气不好。The weather won't be good next Sunday.

zhège dìfāng zhēn hǎo 这个地方真好。This place is really good.
