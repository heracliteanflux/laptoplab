2.26 - Discuss rental details#

air conditioner                              kōng  tiáo           空调
balcony/porch                                yáng  tái            阳台
color                                        yán   sè             颜色
convenience store                            biàn  lì    diàn     便利店🏪
drawing/painting/picture                     huà   r              画儿 (a painting 一张画儿 yì zhāng huà r)
garden                                       huā   yuán           花园
main entrance / gate                         dà    mén            大门🚪
of course / certainly / definitely / sure    dāng  rán            当然👍
on the wall                                  qiáng shàng          墙上
mirror                                       jìng  zi             镜子🪞
neighborhood                                 xiǎo  qū             小区
sir                                          xiān  shēng/sheng    先生🧑‍🏫
wall                                         qiáng                墙🧱
wardrobe                                     yī    guì            衣柜

inside                                       lǐ                   里
outside                                      wài                  外
on                                           shàng                上

old (place)                                  lǎo                  老
safe                                         ān    quán           安全🔐

to (ex)change/replace/swap/convert           huàn                 换
to look (at)                                 kàn                  看👀
to rent                                      zū                   租

yì zhāng chuáng 一张床 a bed
yì zhāng zhuōzi 一张桌子 a table

便利店很重要。(biànlìdiàn hěn zhòngyào) Convenience stores are important.

窗户上有一些画儿吗？(chuānghu shàng yǒu yìxiē huàr ma?) Are there some paintings on the window?
厨房里有什么？(chúfáng lǐ yǒu shénme?) What's in the kitchen?
厨房里有洗碗机。(chúfáng lǐ yǒu xǐwǎnjī) There's a dishwasher in the kitchen.
厨房里有一些窗户吗？(chúfáng lǐ yǒu yìxiē chuānghu ma?) Are there some windows in the kitchen?
厨房里有桌子。(chúfáng lǐ yǒu zhuōzi) There's a table in the kitchen.
大门很新。(dàmén hěn xīn) The main entrance is new.
当然，你可以带家具。(dāngrán, nǐ kěyǐ dài jiājù) Of course, you can bring the furniture.
当然，你可以买新家具。(dāngrán, nǐ kěyǐ mǎi xīn jiājù) Of course, you can buy new furniture.
当然，卧室很大。(dāngrán, wòshì hěn dà) Of course, the bedroom is big.
公寓里有大阳台。(gōngyù lǐ yǒu dà yángtái) There's a big balcony in the apartment.
花园很大。(huāyuán hěn dà) The garden is big.
花园里没有很多花。(huāyuán lǐ méi yǒu hěnduō huā) There aren't many flowers in the garden.
看，客厅里有一个健身房。(kàn, kètīng lǐ yǒu yígè jiànshēnfáng) Look, there's a gym in the living room.
看这个阳台，非常大。(kàn zhège yángtái, fēicháng dà) Look at this balcony, it's extremely big.
客厅里有电视吗？(kètīng lǐ yǒu diànshì ma?) Is there a tv in the living room?
客厅里有两个沙发。(kètīng lǐ yǒu liǎngge shāfā) There are two sofas in the living room.
客厅里有一个沙发。(kètīng lǐ yǒu yígè shāfā) There is a sofa in the living room.
空调吵不吵？(kōngtiáo chǎo bù chǎo?) Is the air conditioner noisy?
那个阳台比这个阳台小。(nàge yángtái bǐ zhège yángtái xiǎo) That porch is smaller than this porch.

我想租一个房间。(wǒ xiǎng zū yígè fángjiān) I want to rent a room.
我喜欢大镜子。(wǒ xǐhuan dà jìngzi) I like big mirrors.
我需要便利店。(wǒ xūyào biànlìdiàn) I need a convenience store.

这个公寓里有空调吗？(zhège gōngyù lǐ yǒu kōngtiáo ma?) Is there an air conditioner in this apartment?

这个花园真漂亮。(zhège huāyuán zhēn piàoliang) This garden is really beautiful.
