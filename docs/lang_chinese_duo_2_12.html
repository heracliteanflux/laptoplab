Section 2 Unit 12 - Use comparatives

dìdi, duō chī niúròu 弟弟，多吃牛肉。Little brother, eat more beef.
hāi, qǐngjìn 嗨，请进。Hi, come in.
kělè zhēn hǎohē 可乐真好喝。Cola is really good.
nàge gēshǒu tèbié piàoliang 那个歌手特别漂亮。That singer is especially beautiful.
nǎichá bǐ kāfēi hǎohē 奶茶比咖啡好喝。Milk tea is better than coffee.
nàshǒu gē méiyǒu zhèshǒu gē xīn ma? 那首歌没有这首歌新吗？That song isn't as new as this song?
nǐ de shūfáng bǐ wǒde dà 你的书房比我的大。Your study is bigger than mine.
nǐ de shūfáng zhēn piàoliang 你的书房真漂亮。Your study is really beautiful.
nǐ dìdi shì dàxuéshēng ma? 你弟弟是大学生吗？Is your little brother a college student?
nǐ gēge bǐ tāmen dà 你哥哥比他们大。Your brother is older than them.
nǐ xǐhuan kàn shénme diànshì jiémù? 你喜欢看什么电视节目？What tv shows do you like to watch?
nǐmen xiǎng kàn nàge jiémù ma? 你们想看那个节目吗？Do y'all want to watch that show?
niúròu bǐ jīròu hǎochī 牛肉比鸡肉好吃。Beef is better than chicken.
píngguǒ bǐ xīguā hǎochī 苹果比西瓜好吃。Apple is tastier than watermelon.
qǐngjìn, zhè shì wǒ de wòshì 请进，这是我的卧室。Come in, this is my bedroom.
rè kāfēi bǐ guǒzhī hǎohē 热咖啡比果汁好喝。Hot coffee is better than juice.
shūfáng bǐ wòshì dà 书房比卧室大。The study is bigger than the bedroom.
tǔdòu méiyǒu yùmǐ xīnxiān 土豆没有玉米新鲜。Potatoes are not as fresh as corn.
wā, nǐ de fángjiān zhēn liàng 哇，你的房间真亮。Wow, your room is really bright.
wā, nǐ de māo hěn cōngmíng 哇，你的猫很聪明。Wow, you cat is very smart.
wā, nǐmen de zhàopiàn zhēn piàoliang 哇，你们的照片真漂亮。Wow, your photos are really beautiful.
wángmíng bǐ wǒmen dà 王明比我们大。Wang Ming is older than us.
wǒ bǐ nǐ dà 我比你大。I'm older than you.
wǒ bù xiǎng kàn yīngwén jiémù 我不想看英文节目。I don't want to watch English shows.
wǒ de chuáng bǐ shāfā shūfú 我的床比沙发舒服。My bed is more comfortable than the sofa.
wǒ de gǒu méiyǒu nǐde hǎokàn 我的狗没有你的好看。My dog doesn't look as good as yours.
wǒ de māo méiyǒu nǐ de māo cōngmíng 我的猫没有你的猫聪明。My cat isn't as smart as your cat.
wǒ de māo xǐhuan chī yùmǐ 我的猫喜欢吃玉米。My cat likes to eat corn.
wǒ de zhàopiàn méiyǒu nǐ de zhàopiàn hǎokàn 我的照片没有你的照片好看。My photo isn't as good-looking as your photo.
wǒ gēge yǒu yìzhǐ xiǎomāo 我哥哥有一只小猫。My brother has a kitten.
wǒ huì kǎo ròu 我会烤肉。I can grill meat.
wǒ kěyǐ kàn nǐ de shūfáng ma? 我可以看你的书房吗？May I see your study?
wǒ mǎile yìxiē shuǐguǒ, nǐ yào píngguǒ ma? 我买了一些水果，你要苹果吗？I bought some fruit, do you want an apple?
wǒ méiyǒu wǒ dìdi cōngmíng 我没有我弟弟聪明。I'm not as smart as my younger brother.
wǒ xiǎng kàn chúfáng 我想看厨房。I want to see the kitchen.
wǒ xiǎng kàn nǐ de tùzi 我想看你的兔子。I want to see your rabbit.
wǒmen yìqǐ tīngle jǐshǒu gē 我们一起听了几首歌。We listened to several songs together.
xīguā bǐ kělè jiànkāng 西瓜比可乐健康。Watermelon is healthier than cola.
xīguā bǐ píngguǒ guì 西瓜比苹果贵。Watermelon is more expensive than apple.
yú bǐ shūcài jiànkāng ma? 鱼比蔬菜健康吗？Is fish healthier than vegetables?
yùmǐ bǐ tǔdòu xīnxiān ma? 玉米比土豆新鲜吗？Is corn more fresh than potatoes?
zhège dàngāo bù xīnxiān 这个蛋糕不新鲜。This cake isn't fresh.
zhège fángjiān tèbié liàng 这个房间特别亮。This room is especially bright.
zhège gēshǒu fēicháng yǒumíng 这个歌手非常有名。This singer is extremely famous.
zhège wòshì shì wǒde 这个卧室是我的。This bedroom is mine.
zhège xīguā bǐ nàge xīguā zhòng 这个西瓜比那个西瓜重。This watermelon is heavier than that watermelon.
zhè shì tā de xī gē 这是他的新歌。This is his new song.
zhè shì wǒdìdi 这是我弟弟。This is my younger brother.
zhè shì wǒjiārén de zhàopiàn 这是我家人的照片。This is a photo of my family.
zhèshǒu gē fēicháng hǎotīng 这首歌非常好听。This song is extremely good.
zhèxiē rén shì wǒjiārén 这些人是我家人。These people are my family.
zhèzhǒng niúròu bǐ nàzhǒng niúròu xīnxiān ma? Is this kind of beef fresher than that kind of beef?
